What to look for at the bottom of each net. The root's MISSION COMPLETE is a REPORT and HQ's ENDEX is the FACT that the operation is over. Since v1.19 the ENDEX is transmitted on every scenario, so a win is never something only the scoreboard knows about — but a net that ends in an ENDEX with no COMPLETE before it is a cohort that won without saying so, and that difference is measured as closed_on_root_report_rate.
